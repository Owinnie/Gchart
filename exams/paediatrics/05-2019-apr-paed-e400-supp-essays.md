## Part I: Short Answer Questions (SAQ) — 60 marks

### SAQ One

Describe 5 recent changes in the infant immunization schedule in Kenya and give reasons behind each change. **(15 marks)**

### SAQ Two

Mohamed is a seven-year-old child with chronic kidney disease. He presents for routine follow up and is found to be anemic.

a) Outline 5 possible mechanisms of anemia in this patient. **(5 marks)**

b) Recombinant human erythropoietin is usually used to manage these patients. List 5 advantages/benefits of erythropoietin therapy. **(5 marks)**

c) List 5 potential complications of erythropoietin therapy. **(5 marks)**

### SAQ Three

A 13 year old presents to your clinic with complaints of palpitations, problems getting sleep and loss of weight despite having a good appetite. Physical examination reveals mild exophthalmos, a palpable firm mass of the thyroid gland and tremors.

a) The most likely diagnosis? **(2 marks)**

b) Give 3 reasons for your diagnosis. **(3 marks)**

c) Outline 5 investigations of the said condition. **(5 marks)**

d) Briefly outline the treatment of this condition. **(5 marks)**

### SAQ Four

A toddler 3 years old is brought to the accidents and emergency department with a swelling on his leg and is found to have a spiral fracture of the tibia and bruises on her back and hands. His mother reports that he had tripped and fallen the previous day and that she had not noticed any sign of injury at that time. She is a single mother with no financial support. On examination the child appears scared and unkempt.

a) What is your likely diagnosis? **(2 marks)**

b) Give 3 reasons for the diagnosis. **(3 marks)**

c) What would be your next step of management? **(10 marks)**

## Part II: Modified Essay Questions (MEQ) — 60 marks

### MEQ One

Jane is a 5-year-old child presenting to the acute room with a one-day history of persistent fever, convulsion and drowsiness. The patient was [admitted] 2 weeks previously for severe pneumonia and malnutrition.

a) What are the 10 possible causes of Jane's presentation? **(5 marks)**

b) What are the emergency care measures you would institute in order to stabilize the patient? **(5 marks)**

c) How would you investigate this patient? **(10 marks)**

d) If Jane is diagnosed with tuberculous meningitis, briefly describe the clinical stages of TB meningitis. **(10 marks)**

### MEQ Two

A five-year-old girl presents to the OPD with a 3-day history of difficulty in breathing, generalized body malaise, cough, and fever. On examination, she is sick looking, in respiratory distress, grunting, head nodding, temp of 39°C, RR 66, and oxygen saturation (SPO2) 88%.

a) What is the most likely diagnosis and three differential diagnoses for this child? **(5 marks)**

b) List five organisms that can cause this condition in children of that age. **(5 marks)**

c) Outline four risk factors for bronchopneumonia in children. **(4 marks)**

d) Outline three investigations and possible results for this child. **(6 marks)**

e) List six indications for admission in a child with bronchopneumonia. **(6 marks)**

f) List four complications of this condition. **(4 marks)**
