## Part I: Short Answer Questions (SAQs) — 60 marks

### SAQ 1

A 2-year-old infant is noted to have mild cyanosis and assumes a squatting position during walking. He is noted to have increasing fussiness followed by increasing cyanosis, limpness and unresponsiveness.

a. What's the most likely underlying lesion? **(2 marks)**

b. Outline the components of this congenital heart condition. **(4 marks)**

c. List 4 investigations you will conduct to confirm the diagnosis and possible findings. **(4 marks)**

d. Outline the management of a hypercyanotic spell. **(5 marks)**

### SAQ 2

A 3-year-old child is admitted in hospital having ingested an organophosphate pesticide.

a. List 10 clinical signs and symptoms of organophosphate poisoning. **(5 marks)**

b. Briefly outline the immediate steps you would take in her treatment. **(10 marks)**

### SAQ 3

a. List any five clinical features of measles. **(5 marks)**

b. List any five complications of measles. **(5 marks)**

c. How would you treat this child with measles? **(5 marks)**

### SAQ 4

A two year old child is brought in to the casualty unresponsive. You are in charge of triage.

a. How would you approach the immediate emergency care for this child? **(10 marks)**

b. List 10 priority signs in triage of children. **(5 marks)**

## Part II: Modified Essay Questions (MEQ) — 60 marks

### MEQ 1

Baby Sean was born at 36 weeks of gestation with a weight of 2200 gm and the APGAR score was 7/1, 9/5, 9/10 and no complication noted at birth.

a) Outline 10 likely causes of preterm delivery for Baby Sean. **(10 marks)**

b) Baby Sean's mother was encouraged to breastfeed the baby immediately after delivery. Outline 10 advantages of exclusive breast-feeding. **(10 marks)**

c) List five problems that are likely to be encountered during breastfeeding. **(5 marks)**

d) List five contraindications of breastfeeding. **(5 marks)**

### MEQ 2

As a Sub-County head, you are concerned about the nutritional status of children in the area. You are giving a talk to health care workers in the institution and you decide to evaluate factors affecting growth, parameters for assessing nutrition and prevention of malnutrition.

a) List 10 factors that affect growth and development in children. **(10 marks)**

b) List 5 parameters used in assessment of physical growth in children. **(5 marks)**

c) Briefly state how head circumference increases in a normal child from birth. **(5 marks)**

d) Outline 10 strategies applied to prevent malnutrition. **(10 marks)**
