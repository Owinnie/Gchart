## How the papers were separated

The dump was not in paper order — an unlabelled 91-question block came first, the April 2019 essays appeared before *and* after the April 2019 MCQs, and the August 2022 SAQ section appeared twice. Papers were identified by their cover-page headers (course code, term, sitting, session) and by the page footers, which differ by era: the 2016 papers carry "Egerton University is ISO 9001:2008 Certified", the 2018/2019 papers carry "Transforming Lives Through Quality Education", and the 2021/2022 papers carry only a page number.

## Editorial conventions applied to every paper

- **Numbering restored, not invented.** OCR preserved printed question numbers on only some pages of each paper. In every paper, renumbering sequentially from 1 reproduces all surviving printed numbers exactly, which is what establishes that no questions are missing from the middle of a paper. Each file records the numbers that survived.
- **Page furniture removed.** Repeated cover headers, "Page N of M" footers, the ISO/motto footers and mid-question instruction blocks have been stripped out of the question flow. Each paper's header and instructions appear once, at the top.
- **Square brackets mark my completions.** Where OCR cut a line off mid-word or mid-phrase and the missing text is unambiguous, it is supplied in brackets, e.g. `total parenteral [nutrition]`, `pulmonary hypertensi[on]`, `growth hormone [d]eficiency`. Where it is not recoverable, the gap is marked `[…truncated]`.
- **Genuine gaps are flagged, not filled.** Text missing from the source is called out in a blockquote under the affected question rather than guessed at — see 2022 MCQ Q20 (options C and D lost) and 2022 MCQ Q45 (a stray fragment belonging to no question).
- **Only mechanical OCR damage was corrected.** Run-together names were re-spaced (`ductusarteriosus`, `Kingellakingae`, `Necatoramericanus`, `HenochSchonleinPurpura`, `Corpulmonale`) and clear character-level errors fixed (`Staphlococcus`, `perisoteum`, `ceftriazone`, `Bilot spots`, `microcephally`, `Vibro cholera`, and `Dead born`/`New born` for `bone` in the osteomyelitis questions). Awkward exam phrasing was left as written — these are the questions as set, not as they might better have been set.
- **Answers were not added.** No answer key exists in the source. The only two answer marks anywhere in the dump were stray `(True)`/`(False)` annotations on the February 2019 paper (Q4 option B, Q8 option D); they have been moved out of the option text into notes so they don't read as part of the paper.

## Two things worth knowing before you use these

**The 91-question set has no cover page.** It is internally consistent as a single continuous paper (its surviving printed numbers run 1–9, 20–28, 39–47, 58–66, 78–86 and align perfectly with sequential numbering), but 91 questions does not match the 60-question format of every identified paper here, and its content spans both the E300 and E400 syllabi. It is most likely a compiled revision set rather than one sat exam. Its only real numbering fault is in the original: the VSD-closure and Duke-criteria questions were both printed as "78".

**Questions repeat heavily across sittings, often with one option changed.** This matters when revising: an option that is correct in one paper is the distractor in another. Notable repeats:
